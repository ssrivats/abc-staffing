Product Designer

Tru7 Group
Remote · Anywhere Full-time Posted 3d ago

Tru7 Group builds the back-office tools mid-market companies actually want to use — from ledgers to onboarding, we replace spreadsheets with software that doesn't fight you. We're a small, senior team that ships fast and argues about details, because the details are the product.

We're looking for an outcome-oriented Product Designer to join our core team. At Tru7, design isn't a downstream polish pass — you'll be embedded with a product manager and two engineers from day one, shaping what we build and why before a single screen gets drawn.

Our design culture

  • We ship in weeks, not quarters — rough is fine as long as it's real
  • Design critique happens in the open channel, not a private review queue
  • We care more about a workflow feeling obvious than looking impressive
  • Support engineers sit next to designers — you'll hear the complaints firsthand

Recent projects our designers have tackled

  • Rebuilding the reconciliation flow so a task that took 40 minutes takes 4
  • Designing a permissions system that finance teams can actually configure themselves
  • A ground-up rework of onboarding, cutting time-to-first-value from a week to a day

Responsibilities

  • Own end-to-end design for a product area, from problem framing through shipped UI
  • Partner directly with your PM and engineers — you're in standup, not just critique
  • Turn ambiguous, half-formed requests into a scoped, buildable direction
  • Push back on complexity before it ships, not after a support ticket flags it
  • Talk to customers directly; research isn't handed to you as a report

Profile

  • You think in flows and edge cases, not just the happy-path screen
  • Comfortable shipping something 80% right this week over 100% right next quarter
  • Can explain a design decision in plain language to someone who isn't a designer
  • Have worked remotely before and don't need a office to stay accountable

Skills and experience

  • 5+ years designing complex web applications or internal tools
  • Strong craft in Figma — component systems, not just static screens
  • Working knowledge of how your designs actually get built (HTML/CSS fluency is a plus)
  • A portfolio that shows reasoning, not just final shots

Bonus qualifications

  • Experience designing for finance, ops, or other data-dense B2B products
  • Comfortable prototyping in code when Figma is slower than the real thing
  • Have used or contributed to a formal design system before

We're a distributed team across four time zones, fully async by default, with no mandated hours beyond your own team's overlap window. We raised our Series A last year specifically to grow this team — this is a seat at the table, not a backfill.
